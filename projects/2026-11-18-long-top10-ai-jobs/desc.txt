Which jobs can AI already do the most of? Microsoft Research scored US jobs using about 200,000 real Copilot chats, and the top 10 are not the factory jobs you'd expect.

We count down the 10 jobs with the highest "AI applicability" score, from mathematicians (0.386) to the #1 job at 0.492, plus the 2.86 million customer service workers who make one rank bigger than the other nine combined, and the hands-on jobs that score zero. A high score means AI can help with many tasks; the researchers warn it is not a prediction of job loss.
