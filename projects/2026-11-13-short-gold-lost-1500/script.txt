In 2025, gold had its best year since 1979. Then, in January 2026, it kept running. Past 5,000 dollars an ounce. Past 5,500. All the way to a record of about 5,600.

Analysts pointed to a weaker US dollar, tension in the Middle East, and a fear that governments would keep printing money.

Then came the drop. By late June, gold had slipped below 4,000 dollars. Then, on October 30th, it hit about 3,940 dollars, its lowest price since November 2025. And this week, it's back under 4,000.

That's more than 1,500 dollars gone from every ounce. The safe haven lost more than a quarter of its value.
