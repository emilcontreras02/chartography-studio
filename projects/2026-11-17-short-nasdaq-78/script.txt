In March 2000, the Nasdaq hit 5,048. Everyone was sure the internet would change everything. By October 2002, the Nasdaq had fallen 78 percent. About five trillion dollars of market value was gone. Cisco, one of the stars of the boom, fell around 80 percent. Amazon dropped from 113 dollars a share to about 15. Does that mean AI is about to crash? Here's the twist. Nobody knows when a bubble pops, not even the experts. And even more important: a bubble bursting does not mean the technology was fake. Look at the internet. The bubble burst. The internet didn't.
