Chapter one. The Empty Chair.

The filing is short. Jensen Huang resigns as president, chief executive, and board member. Effective immediately.

No reason is given. Not one line.

Colette Kress, the company's chief financial officer since 2013, becomes interim CEO. Harvey Jones becomes the first board chair in Nvidia's history. The board had accepted the resignation the day before.

The night before the news, Nvidia closed at one hundred seventy-three dollars and seventy-three cents a share. Back on October sixth, it had set a record. Two hundred thirty-nine dollars.

Chapter three. The Phone Call.

Over the weekend, the board meets again. On Sunday, November twenty-second, it votes. Unanimously. Bring him back.

Monday, November twenty-third. Eight fifty-five a.m., Eastern. The announcement goes out.

The board says it is satisfied with his candor and ability to lead. It says it received input from senior levels of the United States government. And that his return poses no national security risk.

By the close, Nvidia is up thirty-one point four eight percent. One hundred sixty-five dollars and five cents.
